About me

A data analyst who ships the answer

I'm a data analyst with 2+ years of experience across analytics and low-code engineering.

My approach is simple: understand the decision first, then build the smallest thing that makes it obvious. That usually means cleaning and joining the data properly, modelling it once, and only then designing the visuals — because a beautiful chart built on a shaky model is just a confident mistake.

I care about clarity over decoration: consistent KPI definitions, readable labels, sensible colour, and a layout that a busy stakeholder can read in the first five seconds. And I document what I build, so the work survives after the dashboard ships.
